# System Dynamics Model of Electronics VAT and Plastic Waste in Water

A stock and flow model in STELLA that tests whether VAT collected on electronics can reduce plastic waste from electronics in Turkish waters.

## Overview

Electronics produce plastic waste that ends up in water, while their sales also generate VAT revenue. We modeled how that revenue could fund waste management and recycling, and simulated the effect over 20 years.

## What I did

- Built a stock and flow model in STELLA linking electronics demand, production and VAT revenue to waste management funding, recycling and plastic removal in Turkish waters over a 20 year horizon
- Validated the model with dimensional consistency, extreme condition and behavior reproduction tests, and ran sensitivity analyses showing that funding allocation reduced plastic accumulation far more than VAT rates

## Methods and tools

System dynamics, causal loop and stock and flow diagrams, STELLA, model validation tests, sensitivity analysis.
